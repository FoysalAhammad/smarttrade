## Full description (≤ 4000 chars)

```
Smart Trade is a modern crypto swing-trading journal built for Binance spot traders. Track every entry and exit, see REAL net profit after exchange fees, and read live candlestick charts with your own trading signals — on a clean, dark, fast interface.

WHY TRADERS PICK SMART TRADE
• Live SUI/USDT & multi-coin charts — real-time candlesticks, volume and crosshair OHLC straight from Binance.
• Switch markets in one tap — pick any coin/USDT pair you hold (min. 1 USDT) from the built-in coin selector.
• TradingView-style strategy scripts — write ema, sma, rsi, crossover and plot() lines; your buy/sell signals render on the chart.
• Fee-aware P&L — every profit figure is shown AFTER the real Binance taker fee (0.075% per leg, 0.15% round trip), auto-fetched from your API key.
• Two swing methods — Sell-first (short the dip) or Buy-first (ride the rise) with auto re-entry / take-profit targets, or switch targets OFF for manual journal trading.
• Full trade history — entry, exit, amount, fee, net profit and wallet balance snapshots for every completed cycle, grouped by date.
• Portfolio & daily P&L — multi-coin balances with live USDT valuation and all-time / today profit breakdown.
• Demo paper trading — practice with a virtual wallet and real market prices before risking funds.
• Account-level security — your API keys are encrypted in the device keystore (Android Keystore / Keychain), never leave your phone, and are used read-only. No withdrawals, ever.

PERFECT FOR
• Swing traders who want an honest journal (true fees, true P&L)
• Binance spot users who track SUI, BTC, ETH and other USDT pairs
• Anyone learning technical analysis with candlestick charts and indicators

DISCLAIMER
Smart Trade is a journal and analysis tool. It does not place trades and is not financial advice. Crypto trading involves risk.

Privacy-first: no accounts, no trackers, no ads — data stays on your device.
```
