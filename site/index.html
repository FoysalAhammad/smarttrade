Trading methods

Two proven swing playbooks

Both fully fee-aware. Targets can be switched off for fully manual journal trading.

METHOD 1 · SELL FIRST

Sold the top → buy the dip back

Already hold coins → sell at market → wait for a dip → re-buy lower. Profit is counted in extra coins: re-buy price < sell price.

sell at market → arm lower target → re-buy lower → extra coins kept ✅
METHOD 2 · BUY FIRST

Buy the dip → sell the rise

Buy at market, arm a higher take-profit target, and exit into strength. Profit is counted in USDT after fees.

buy at market → arm higher target → sell above → USDT profit secured ✅

Default fee 0.075% per leg (0.15% round trip) — auto-fetched from your account when connected.
