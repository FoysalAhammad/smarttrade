## The30-second pitch

- 📈 **Chart** — candles, volume, crosshair OHLC, 1m→4H, two-finger zoom & pan
- 🧠 **Your signals** — `ema`, `sma`, `rsi`, `crossover`, `plot()` rendered live
- 🪙 **Any USDT market** — tap the pair name; coins ≥ 1 USDT listed first
- 🧮 **True P&L** — every number shown **after** the exchange fee
- 🔒 **Zero telemetry** — no account, no server, no analytics, ever
