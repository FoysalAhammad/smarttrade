Trading methods

Two proven swing playbooks

Both fully fee-aware. Targets can be switched off for manual journal trading.

METHOD 1 · SELL FIRST

Sold the top → buy the dip back

Sell holdings at market, arm a lower re-entry target, and accumulate extra coins when price comes back. Profit is measured in coins.

sell price → arm lower target → re-buy below → coins gained ✅
METHOD 2 · BUY FIRST

Bought low → sell the rise

Buy at market, arm a higher take-profit target, and exit into strength. Profit is measured in USDT, fee deducted.

buy → arm higher target → sell above → USDT gained ✅

Default fee 0.075% per leg (0.15% round trip) — auto-fetched from your account when connected.
