Strategies

Two Swing Methods

Choose the strategy that fits your style — both fully supported with fee-aware P&L.

01
Sell First

Sell → Re-buy the Dip

Already holding? Log your sell, wait for the dip, buy back lower. Profit shows in extra coins — perfect for ranging markets.

profit = sellPrice − reentryPrice → extra SUI
02
Buy First

Buy → Sell the Rise

Buy at market, wait for the rise, sell higher. Profit shows in USDT (expressed in SUI at entry) — ideal for trending markets.

profit = sellPrice − buyPrice → USDT (net of fees)
